ADNOC HQ — Unified Command Center

Case Studies — Adnoc Hq Command Center. Premium LED display solutions by Brightluxx in Dubai, UAE.

The corporate command center required a massive video wall capable of displaying real-time operational data from 40+ sources simultaneously. The display needed 24/7 operation reliability, front serviceability for zero-downtime maintenance, and integration with 6 different data visualization platforms.

A Fine Pitch LED wall at P1.2 was specified for the optimal balance of resolution, reliability, and cost at 3m+ viewing distance. Redundant power supplies and signal processing ensured zero single points of failure. Custom input matrix allowed any source to be displayed at any position and size on the wall.

Location: Abu Dhabi, UAE
