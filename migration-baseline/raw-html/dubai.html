Dubai Signage Regulations & Power

Dubai Municipality Signage Permit

All external LED signage in Dubai requires a Dubai Municipality advertisement permit covering size, placement and structural safety. We prepare the drawings and calculations your PRO submits.

DED Signboard Guidelines

Onshore trade signage must follow Dubai Economy signboard rules for trade-name display. Our designs are prepared to pass DED review the first time.

RTA Brightness Limits

Screens visible from Dubai roads must respect RTA luminance and content-motion limits. We fit ambient light sensors that auto-dim outdoor screens at night to stay compliant.

DEWA Power Requirements

Large outdoor LED screens typically need a dedicated three-phase DEWA supply with approved load calculations. We size the power requirement, provide load schedules and coordinate with your electrical contractor.
